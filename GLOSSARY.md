# NotToday

A personal daily planner for one person: things to do show up on a day, can be pushed off with "not today", and some arrive as suggestions found in WhatsApp messages.

## Language

**Task**:
Something to do, shown on exactly one day (its due day) until it is done or deferred.
_Avoid_: Todo, item, reminder

**Today**:
The list of Tasks and Birthdays due on the current calendar day, in the user's chosen order.
_Avoid_: Daily view, dashboard

**Not today**:
The action of moving an unfinished Task off Today, to tomorrow, a picked date, or Someday.
_Avoid_: Snooze, postpone, reschedule

**Slip**:
One day a Task was due and was not done. A Task that is not done by the end of its day rolls to the next day and gains a Slip.
_Avoid_: Overdue, missed day

**Someday**:
A holding place for Tasks with no due date; they do not appear on Today until given one.
_Avoid_: Backlog, later, inbox

**Birthday**:
A person's yearly date that appears on Today every year, with a **Wished** mark that resets each year.
_Avoid_: Anniversary, event

**Wished**:
The mark, with its date, that the user has wished a person a happy birthday for a given year.
_Avoid_: Done, greeted

**Belated**:
A Birthday whose day has passed without a Wished mark, still shown on Today for up to 3 days.
_Avoid_: Overdue, missed

**Chat**:
A WhatsApp conversation, one-to-one or group, that messages are scanned from unless the user has switched it off.
_Avoid_: Thread, group, contact

**Inbox**:
The queue of Suggestions waiting for the user to accept or dismiss.
_Avoid_: Notifications, feed

**Suggestion**:
A possible Task or Birthday found in a WhatsApp message, never added to Today until the user accepts it.
_Avoid_: Detection, auto-task, alert
